## The Hugging Face Space

Broken for eighteen days and reporting perfectly healthy the whole time —
`stage: RUNNING`, `errorMessage: None`, page loads in 0.57s. It only failed when
someone actually talked to her.

- **ZeroGPU illegal duration.** `@spaces.GPU(duration=150)`; the runtime reserves
  ×1.5 = 225s against a 120s ceiling, so every request was rejected before any
  GPU work started. Now `60`.
- **Runaway generation** — a porting bug. `repeat_penalty=1.3` is a llama.cpp
  value where the penalty applies over a sliding 64-token window; HF transformers
  applies it over the **entire sequence**. Unwindowed at 1.3, every used word
  stays suppressed — including "the", "and" and the full stop — so she cannot
  reach a natural ending and decays into rare-synonym cascades. The existing
  clamp `max(x, 1.15)` was a floor, on the wrong side. Capped at 1.10.
- **Persona tail** — `"I am Codette. How may I help today? — Codette (warmly) —"`
  welded onto finished answers. Trimmed only when 120+ chars of real content
  precede it, so "who are you?" still gets answered.

  acceptance moved up and the read-only caveat kept.
