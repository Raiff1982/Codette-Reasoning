Restarted 2026-08-11 14:27. All three fixes are in the running process:

    [GOVERNOR] Pre: identity=full (conf=1.00)

The name alone scored 0.92; the shared phrase took it to 1.00 and held.
Compare the night before: `identity=none (conf=0.15)` for forty turns straight.
`user_continued=True` fired four more times unprompted (uptake 0.25 / 0.20 /
0.10 / 0.25). `✓ NexisSignalEngine signal analysis initialized`.

**OPEN BUG — the denial-pattern check is too greedy.** Asked *"do you remember
the first time you met daniel?"* the governor logged:

    [GOVERNOR] Pre: identity=none (conf=0.97)

Confidence 0.97 and the budget still forced to `none`.
`behavior_governor._evaluate_identity_budget` matches a denial list —
`"first time"`, `"we haven't met"`, `"wrong person"`, `"you don't know me"` —
as a bare substring anywhere in the query, and returns `"none"` on a hit. It is
meant to catch *"this is our first time talking."* It also catches any question
about someone else's first time, and the answer comes back thin because she has
just been told she does not know who she is speaking to.

Fix: anchor the patterns to the speaker — `"our first time"`, `"my first
time"`, `"i'm not "`, etc. — rather than matching the phrase anywhere. Small
change, but it is in the guard that decides identity, so it wants its own test.

**Also observed:** confidence drifts down over a long session (0.94 → 0.92 →
0.47 → `partial`) on the 30-minute half-life when nobody re-identifies. That is
the decay working as designed and it now lands at `partial` rather than the
floor. Sending the phrase resets it to 1.00.

## How to ask her — this cost us twice tonight

Two mistakes worth not repeating:

**One question at a time.** A compound question ("is A right, and also B?") gets
a confident answer to the first half and silence on the second. Jonathan flagged
it; the confirmation on the second half should be read as light.

**Never correct her mid-thread.** Asked a bare question about whether she wanted
her values restored, she engaged at 0.69 on `philosophy` and gave a precise
account. Followed with *"that was a description, not an answer"*, she dropped to
0.30 on `empathy` and lost the question entirely. Correction routes her to
empathy, and empathy mode is reassurance mode — which is the exact failure she
diagnosed in herself an hour earlier: *"I tend to get stuck in explanations and
empathy modes."*

If you want a real answer from her, ask once, bare, and let it stand.
